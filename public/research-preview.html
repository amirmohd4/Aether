India-wide vision · Public research MVP · Synthetic environment

Government work should move as one connected journey.

Aether GovOS is being built as an administrative execution layer: it helps coordinate the work that happens after a request starts—requirements, evidence checks, department handoffs, recoverable failures, human review and outcome verification—across existing systems.

Important boundary: This is an early research MVP, not a live government service. The public demo uses fictional cases and simulated department responses. It does not connect to official records, submit real applications, issue permits or benefits, or make legal decisions. Do not enter real personal data or upload confidential records. No government endorsement is claimed.

The goal is a credible demonstration of the full vision—not a claim that every department is already integrated or that production results have been proven.

The problem we are testing

Digitising one form does not coordinate the entire process.

A citizen or business may begin in one portal, supply evidence, wait for checks from different teams, answer follow-up requests, and still have no clear view of what is blocked or who must act next. Officers and integrators may face repeated data entry, fragmented status, manual chasing and recovery after systems fail. The exact pain differs by service and jurisdiction; we need practitioners to validate it.

The Aether concept

From a request to a traceable, verified status.

Understand request→Find requirements→Build work graph→Coordinate tasks→Verify & reconcile→Human authority→Traceable status
MAP

Know the dependencies

Turn a goal into tasks, owners, prerequisites and safe points to pause instead of treating every case as a simple checklist.

COORDINATE

Run permitted work

Model tasks that can happen in parallel, preserve case context, and recover from temporary failures without creating duplicate side effects.

VERIFY

Show why a status changed

Keep a trace of checks, source or simulation, exceptions and reconciliation. Never claim success just because a request was sent.

GOVERN

Respect authority

Stop for statutory decisions, inspections, disputed evidence and other reserved actions. An authorised person remains responsible.

Aether is intended to complement—not replace—existing government portals, case-management tools, registries and official systems. A workflow can only use integrations that its owner has authorised.

CASE DEMO-2048 · SYNTHETIC

“Help me understand and coordinate what is needed to open a restaurant.”

Aether first maps the intended outcome, identifies candidate requirements and makes uncertainty visible. In a real deployment, jurisdiction-specific rules and every connector would require validation and permission.

  1. 1
    Understand the request

    Capture business type and location at the minimum necessary level; ask a clarification question if the target service is ambiguous.

  2. 2
    Build a dependency-aware plan

    Map illustrative premises, business and food-safety checks. Mark which can run in parallel and which depend on earlier evidence.

  3. 3
    Coordinate approved checks

    In this public demo, simulated systems return fictional statuses. An authorised live pilot would require the system owner's approval and a supported interface.

  4. 4
    Resolve gaps and exceptions

    If a document is missing or a system times out, keep completed work, explain the blocker and resume safely after the issue is resolved.

  5. 5
    Pause for required human action

    Inspection, professional certification or a legal decision stays with the authorised person wherever the real process requires it.

  6. 6
    Reconcile the outcome

    Show evidence references, task state and next action. The public preview records only a simulated outcome—not a real licence or approval.
