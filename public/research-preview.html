What happens after a form is submitted?

Aether explores an execution layer for complex workflows: coordinating permitted digital tasks, evidence checks, dependencies, retries and exceptions—while keeping statutory decisions and required physical actions with authorised people.

Prototype, not a live government service. This preview uses simulated department systems. It does not access protected government records, submit real applications, issue official decisions or certificates, or prove real-world time or cost savings. Never upload real identity documents or confidential case records.

The hypothesis

Digitising intake is only one part of service delivery.

01 · COORDINATE

Map the work

Represent requirements, dependencies and departmental handoffs as a case-specific workflow instead of a loose checklist.

02 · EXECUTE

Handle permitted digital tasks

Run independent steps, track evidence, retry recoverable failures and surface exceptions without claiming authority the system does not have.

03 · VERIFY

Keep people in control

Record outcomes and pause at statutory decisions, disputed evidence, inspections and other actions that require an authorised human.
